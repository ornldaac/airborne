# SEG Workshop 2026

## Workshop on Remote Sensing and Critical Mineral Discovery

**Date:** September 29, 2026, Salt Lake City, Utah

**Host**: University of Utah


## Overview
The [Workshop on Remote Sensing and Critical Mineral Discovery](https://atmos.utah.edu/critical_minerals_imaging_workshop/index.php) brought together experts from industry, academia, and government to explore the use of hyperspectral and remote sensing data in domestic critical mineral discovery.

Participants learned about hyperspectral data and its applications, as well as the available datasets for critical mineral identification. Sessions explored perspectives on integrating spectral data with other vital geoscience resources, such as airborne geophysics, geologic mapping, and geochemical databases. The workshop included hands-on training to access and use data from the Geological Earth Mapping Experiment (GEMx) airborne campaign and Earth Surface Mineral Dust Source Investigation (EMIT) mission through NASA Earthdata Search and the JPL GEMx portal.
